My research interests lie broadly in the intersections of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Visualization (VIS), and Extended Reality (XR/AR/VR), with a particular focus on immersive data visualization and interaction. Through my research, I aim to design next-generation immersive interfaces and spatial computing systems that fundamentally transform how humans perceive and interact with data.

Previously, I completed my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering at HKUST, advised by Prof. Xiaojuan Ma.

My three research themes:

📊 Interaction with Data Visualization
Designing human-data interaction tools that make data visualization more intuitive, expressive, and accessible.
🥽 Interaction Techniques in AR/VR
Creating efficient interaction methods for immersive applications, from situated visualization to spatial computing.
🤖 Interactive Human-AI Experiences
Exploring ways to enhance how humans collaborate with AI systems through better interfaces and interaction design.
